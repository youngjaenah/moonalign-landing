Privacy Policy

MoonAlign has no accounts and processes your shooting plans and photos on your device. Only what a feature needs is sent to the service that provides it.

Effective date: October 6, 2026

YJLAB ("we") makes and operates the MoonAlign app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android (the "app"). This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is sent and how long it is kept.

Summary

  • MoonAlign has no sign-up or login. We don't ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools. We don't track or sell your activity.
  • Shooting plans, photos and the camera view are processed on your device. They are not sent to our server.
  • For features such as maps, terrain and live aircraft positions, the app sends coordinates or search terms to the service that provides that feature. Those services use them only to answer the request.
  • Our server keeps only the minimum needed to issue map keys, such as a random install ID.

Information kept on our server

To hand out map keys securely, the app talks to a server we operate on Cloudflare Workers. The server checks that the request comes from a genuine copy of the app before issuing a key, and keeps only the following.

InformationRetention
Install identifier — a random ID the app creates on first launch (Android) or an Apple App Attest key ID (iOS, Mac), with its public key. It is not a hardware ID or advertising ID.As long as the installation exists (overwritten on re-registration)
Request counts — daily requests per install identifier and per IP address (abuse prevention)Up to 48 hours (2 hours for IP addresses)
Subscription check result — a hash of the Google Play purchase token with the product and expiry time (Android subscribers)Up to 6 hours

To confirm the app is genuine, the server receives a verdict from Apple (App Attest) or Google (Play Integrity). Android subscriptions are confirmed by looking up the purchase token with Google Play. For iOS and Mac subscriptions, the server only verifies the App Store transaction signature and does not keep it. Your location, shooting plans and search terms never reach this server.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payment for the app and the Photorealistic 3D subscription is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment method or billing details. You can manage subscriptions and request refunds in each store.

Sync and backup

  • iCloud (iOS, Mac): Shooting plans and My landmarks are saved in your own iCloud Drive, and your own Google API key in iCloud Keychain, so they sync across devices on the same Apple Account.
  • Google Drive (Android, optional): Only if you turn it on in Settings, shooting plans and My landmarks are saved to an app-only folder in your own Drive. The app can't access any other Drive files.
  • Android backup: If device backup is on, app settings and shooting plans may be included in Android's standard backup. Keys and install identifiers are excluded from backup.

This data is stored in your own account, and we can't see it.

Share links

A link created with Share contains the shooting plan itself, including target and shooting site coordinates, the date and notes. That part comes after the # in the address, so it is never sent to the website's server. However, anyone who receives the link can see its contents, so check who you share it with.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices and deletion

  • You can turn off location, photo, camera and notification permissions at any time in your device settings. Only the related features stop working.
  • Deleting the app removes its data from your device. Data synced to iCloud or Google Drive can be deleted in each service's settings (Drive: Settings → Manage apps).
  • To have your install identifier deleted from our server, contact us at the address below. Since there are no user accounts, sending the request from Home → Help & About → Email support in the app helps us find it, because the app version and device details are filled in for you.

Changes to this policy

If the information the app uses changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
